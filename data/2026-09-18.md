TL;DR: 半连续Au薄膜与压紧CrO2粉末虽形态、维度和微观输运不同，但都表明电学相关网络只是物理接触网络的条件依赖子集，并存在“链接启用”和“链接重加权”两种可相互转换的重构模式。

Motivation: 探究强非均匀导体中输运性质是否仅由几何连通性决定，并通过比较不同材料体系寻找网络层面的共性原理。

Method: 对比半连续Au薄膜与压紧CrO2粉末：在Au薄膜中改变偏压并测量低温输运与磁阻；在CrO2粉末压实体中施加磁场、改变温度和测量电流；用Cohn定理分析局部电阻变化对整体电学响应的贡献。

Result: Au薄膜近几何逾渗阈值时，增大偏压可逆地将低温响应从激活型最近邻跳跃与负磁阻转变为类弱局域化与正磁阻；CrO2压实体中磁场主要改变已有颗粒间结的自旋相关电阻并重排竞争电流路径，温度与测量电流还可扩展或耗尽活跃网络。Cohn定理给出统一敏感性原理：局部电阻变化对测量响应的影响正比于受影响链接所承载的电流。

Conclusion: 电学相关网络是物理接触网络的条件依赖子集；网络重构可归纳为链接启用与链接重加权两种极限但可相互转换的模式；因此仅凭几何连通性不足以解释强非均匀导体的输运行为。

Abstract: Despite fundamental differences in morphology, dimensionality, and microscopic transport, semicontinuous Au films and compacted CrO2 powders converge on a common network-level principle: the electrically relevant network is a condition-dependent subset of the physical contact network. In Au films near the geometrical percolation threshold, increasing bias reversibly shifts the low-temperature response from activated nearest-neighbour hopping with negative magnetoresistance to a weak-localization-like regime with positive magnetoresistance, consistent with broader electronic participation of the fixed island network. In CrO2 powder compacts, magnetic field mainly changes the spin-dependent resistances of existing intergranular junctions and thereby reorders competing current paths; temperature and measuring current can additionally expand or deplete the active network. Cohn's theorem provides a compact sensitivity principle for both cases: local resistance changes influence the measured response in proportion to the current carried by the affected links. The comparison identifies two limiting but interconvertible modes of reconfiguration - link enabling and link reweighting - and shows why geometrical connectivity alone is insufficient for interpreting transport in strongly inhomogeneous conductors.
