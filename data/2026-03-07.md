ALD TaN does not exhibit infrared absorption due to free carriers, even at elevated temperatures, demonstrating its insulating nature, which is required for the tunnel barrier of the JJ in quantum applications. The results of transmission electron microscopy, including selected area electron diffraction, and X-ray diffraction are also discussed. Sputter depth-profile X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) shows an N/Ta ratio of ~1.2 throughout the film. The lower band gap, low roughness, and thermal stability of ALD TaN compared to AlOx suggest the possibility of fabricating JJs with thicker barriers while achieving critical current densities required for qubits, better control of thickness and composition, reduced topography, and resistance to aging.
